# CatReNet 1.1.5

## New features

- A toolbar button, the leftmost one, shows or hides the input side bar, as in SplitsTree and PhyloParallelograms. The
  side bar reopens at the width it had, and dragging the divider shut or open updates the button.

## Bug fixes

- In the dark theme, copying the network produced a white drawing on a dark background. The image is now always dark
  on a white background.
- In the dark theme, a white strip showed along the right and bottom edges of the network pane.
- The Copy item of the Export button is now named Copy Image while the Network tab is showing.

## Known issues

- Long edges in the layered layout may pass through other nodes

## iPhone and iPad app

- CatReNet is also available as an app for iPhone and iPad, see
  <https://husonlab.github.io/mobile-apps/>. Changes that concern the app only are described with each
  update of the app, not here
